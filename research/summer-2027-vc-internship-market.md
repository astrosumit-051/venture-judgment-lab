## What the live Bessemer application actually tests

Bessemer makes the recruiting evidence concrete. Beyond a résumé, GPA, transcript, and LinkedIn profile, it requests:

- a 1,000-character explanation of how lived experience shapes the applicant's views on technology, entrepreneurship, and society;
- a 1,200-character industry trend and an investable Seed/Series A company exemplifying it;
- a one-minute video explaining motivation and intended learning; and
- optionally, one to three peers with exceptional founder potential and evidence for that view.

The role states that it values technology curiosity, leadership, communication and persuasion, determination, and reasonable business fluency. These prompts directly validate the Lab's Sector Discovery Cycle, Founder Evidence, sourcing practice, concise writing, and oral-defense work. They also make the inaccurate old résumé unsafe to use: the current 3.2 GPA must be reported truthfully, and unsupported diligence, modeling, or investment-attribution claims must be removed.

## F-1 work authorization implications

This section is planning information, not legal advice. The learner should rely on Centre's Designated School Official for their specific record.

### Summer 2027

- CPT is the normal path for an eligible F-1 student internship during the degree. Federal guidance says CPT must be integral to the curriculum and authorized by the DSO on a new Form I-20 before work begins. [ICE practical-training guidance](https://www.ice.gov/sevis/practical-training).
- Centre says off-campus internship work requires CPT and must relate to the student's field of study; it directs students to its DSO. [Centre international-student career guidance](https://careers.centre.edu/channels/international-students/) and [Centre International Student Services](https://www.centre.edu/admission-aid/international-applicants/international-student-services).
- A firm does not file an H-1B petition merely to host a CPT-authorized summer intern, but an employer may still choose not to accept CPT. Every posting must therefore be screened for explicit authorization restrictions.
- Bessemer's application provides a student-visa response and asks for the details, so F-1 status is not an automatic application blocker. It does not guarantee acceptance or any later sponsorship.

### After graduation

- Standard post-completion OPT is student-held work authorization, but the work must be directly related to the degree.
- A 24-month STEM OPT extension requires a qualifying CIP code, a bona fide employer enrolled and in good standing with E-Verify, at least 20 hours/week, compensation commensurate with similarly situated workers, and an individualized Form I-983 training plan directly relating the work to the STEM degree. Small funds may be unwilling or unable to meet these obligations. [USCIS practical-training policy](https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-2-part-f-chapter-5), [DHS Form I-983 overview](https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/form-i-983-overview).
- The learner should obtain written confirmation of the exact I-20 CIP code and Centre's CPT/OPT process. A general statement that “Data Science is STEM” is not enough.
- Longer-term H-1B or permanent-residence sponsorship is a separate employer decision. The full-time target-firm universe must eventually distinguish firms that merely accept current OPT from firms willing and able to support a longer runway.
