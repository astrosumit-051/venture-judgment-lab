## Decision-relevant conclusion

**A 2026–2027 opening is not currently verified. Do not treat September 11, 2026 as an application deadline.** Dorm Room Fund's recruiting page says applications are open and gives September 11 at 11:59 p.m. ET for the Philadelphia & Southeast team, but the page never names a year or academic cycle. Its official “Apply Now” link opens an Airtable page titled **“2025-2026 DRF Investment Partner Application”** whose only application-specific content says that application is closed. An official Dorm Room Fund article published August 27, 2025 advertised the same September 11 and September 25 dates, which strongly indicates the undated recruiting page is still describing the 2025 cycle rather than a live 2026–2027 cycle. ([recruiting page](https://join.dormroomfund.com/), [linked Airtable form](https://airtable.com/appCfqw6GzgU4uyqg/shrJDjG8KU6jHuwte), [August 27, 2025 Dorm Room Fund article](https://wp.dormroomfund.com/addiction-can-be-reprogrammed/))

The opportunity would be a high-value investing milestone if a new cycle opens: the published role provides genuine sourcing, evaluation, diligence, and investment-decision responsibility. It is also a 10–15-hour weekly commitment lasting until graduation, so it should replace most Venture Judgment Lab practice rather than stack on top. It must **not** be classified as paid employment or a qualifying internship unless Dorm Room Fund confirms compensation and legal classification in writing; its public material establishes neither.
