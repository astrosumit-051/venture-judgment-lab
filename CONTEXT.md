# Venture Judgment Lab

The shared language for a living apprenticeship designed to develop and demonstrate the learner's readiness for an early-stage technology investing career.

## Language

**Destination Event**:
The learner accepts an investing role at a highly regarded US early-stage technology venture capital firm. This ends the apprenticeship but not the learner's longer pursuit of investment excellence.
_Avoid_: Final day, becoming the best VC

**Target Firm**:
A respected US technology investment organization with meaningful pre-seed through Series A activity, strong ethics and founder references, direct access to accomplished investors, and roles that provide real exposure to sourcing, founder meetings, diligence, and investment reasoning. YC, a16z, and Pear VC are examples rather than a fixed qualifying list.
_Avoid_: Big-name fund, famous VC, brand-name firm

**Qualifying Investing Role**:
A full-time paid investment-team position with recurring participation in sourcing, founder evaluation, diligence, and investment discussions. Scout programs, fellowships, internships, platform roles, and portfolio-support roles are milestones but do not trigger the Destination Event.
_Avoid_: Any VC job, adjacent role

**Internship Readiness Gate**:
The live Summer 2027 application period, already underway by August 5, 2026. Readiness and applications now proceed concurrently because some programs opened in September 2025 and strong exact-fit roles are currently open.
_Avoid_: March 2027 deadline, future recruiting season

**General CPT Eligibility**:
Centre College's confirmation that the learner may be eligible to use curricular practical training for a qualifying experience. It is not employer- or role-specific CPT authorization and must not be represented as permission to begin a particular internship.
_Avoid_: CPT approved, work authorized for the internship

**Investing Milestone**:
A student investing apprenticeship, fellowship, scout role, or live decision-making program that materially develops evidence and access but does not satisfy the paid Qualifying Internship definition.
_Avoid_: Qualifying Internship, résumé badge

**Qualifying Internship**:
A paid Summer 2027 early-stage technology investing internship with direct exposure to sourcing, founder evaluation, diligence, and the investment team. Accelerator and emerging-manager investing roles can qualify; startup, consulting, and finance internships are fallback outcomes.
_Avoid_: Any finance internship, any VC-adjacent internship

**Venture Judgment Lab**:
The living apprenticeship and evidence record that runs until the Destination Event.
_Avoid_: Course, content library

**Private Learning Record**:
The complete dated record of raw analyses, predictions, errors, reflections, and potentially sensitive company information; it is private by default and preserves original work alongside later updates.
_Avoid_: Public portfolio, polished persona

**Public Portfolio Artifact**:
A deliberately selected and sanitized thesis, memo, prediction result, or other work product approved for recruiting use without confidential information.
_Avoid_: Raw learning record, automatic publication

**Daily Judgment Loop**:
A weekday practice combining the Daily Brief, one rapid early-stage company screen, and one falsifiable prediction about what happens next.
_Avoid_: Daily content feed, reading list

**Normal Week**:
The Lab's default 11.5-hour practice mode: five 90-minute Daily Judgment Loops plus one four-hour flexible weekend block for a Weekly Underwrite, recruiting, and reflection.
_Avoid_: Mandatory clock-time schedule, unlimited weekly workload

**Monthly Calibration Week**:
The once-per-calendar-month 11.5-hour mode that preserves all five Daily Judgment Loops while shortening the Weekly Underwrite to make room for a Calibration Review.
_Avoid_: Extra calibration hours, hindsight rewrite week

**Recruiting Surge**:
A temporary practice mode used when a dated application, interview, or relationship commitment cannot fit within the Normal Week's recruiting block. It replaces selected practice while preserving at least three Daily Judgment Loops and never expands the week beyond 12 hours.
_Avoid_: Always-on recruiting, hours added above the practice budget

**Exam Mode**:
The learner-activated three-hour minimum practice mode for exam-heavy or unusually difficult academic weeks. It preserves one Daily Brief, one Snapshot Judgment and Forecast, urgent recruiting when present, and a restart reflection without creating catch-up debt.
_Avoid_: Failed Normal Week, hidden backlog, complete disengagement

**Daily Brief**:
Four curated readings within a combined 55-minute cap: one current signal, one durable investing insight, one cross-domain input, and one career or freeflow piece. Every selection has a stated teaching purpose and passes source, access, freshness, duplication, and preservation gates before it feeds the day's company screen, Forecast, professional development, or reflection.
_Avoid_: News dump, popular-link roundup

**Reading Record**:
The immutable assignment metadata and dated append-only events for every Daily Brief selection, including source identity and status, teaching purpose, learner response, corrections, replacements, linked artifacts, and later usefulness. Sites presents the archive while the Private Learning Record preserves its canonical history without rewriting the original record.
_Avoid_: Bookmarks, mutable recommendations list

**Weekly Underwrite**:
An end-to-end, question-led analysis of one early-stage company that investigates three Load-Bearing Questions, integrates traceable supporting and disconfirming evidence, and appends a deeper Practice Disposition and Decision Delta without rewriting the original Snapshot Judgment.
_Avoid_: Weekend book report, checklist review

**Practice Disposition**:
A decision about the next use of scarce Lab attention: Pursue for immediate diligence or relationship work, Watch for a named uncertainty or trigger, or Pass until a stated condition changes. It is not a simulated investment-committee vote or a prediction that the company succeeds or fails.
_Avoid_: Startup grade, investment recommendation

**Load-Bearing Question**:
One of exactly three company-specific questions in a Weekly Underwrite whose answer could materially change the learner's Practice Disposition, confidence, or causal investment case.
_Avoid_: Generic diligence category, comprehensive checklist item

**Evidence Ledger**:
The claim-linked record separating observations, sources, source roles, dates, reliability limits, direction, and learner inference during an Underwrite.
_Avoid_: Link dump, undifferentiated research notes

**Decision Delta**:
The dated explanation of what a deeper Underwrite retained, changed, or reversed from the original Snapshot Judgment and which evidence caused the update.
_Avoid_: Rewritten first impression, unexplained confidence change

**Calibration Review**:
A monthly comparison of prior predictions, investment judgments, sourcing results, and analytical mistakes with later evidence. It preserves the original view, resolves eligible Forecasts without changing their odds, measures probabilistic accuracy with a Brier score, records a changed decision rule, and names the next practice that applies it.
_Avoid_: Progress recap, hindsight rewrite

**Forecast**:
A falsifiable claim about a future event with an explicit probability, time horizon, supporting evidence, and disconfirming condition.
_Avoid_: Prediction without odds, confident narrative

**Second-Order Map**:
A causal map tracing an event through an explicit direct consequence, actor response, and resulting third-order behavior or equilibrium, then stress-testing bottlenecks, incentives, suppliers, customers, substitutes, regulation, adjacent domains, and disconfirming evidence.
_Avoid_: Trend list, thematic brainstorming

**Snapshot Judgment**:
The learner's 20-minute Independent First Pass on an early-stage company, committing a causal thesis, venture-scale mechanism, Practice Disposition, confidence, crux, evidence, disconfirming signal, and top unknown before deep research.
_Avoid_: Final recommendation, first impression rewritten later

**Countercase**:
The strongest evidence-based argument against the learner's current investment view, including a premortem of how and why the company could fail.
_Avoid_: Token risks section, weak devil's advocate

**Founder Evidence**:
Observable behavior supporting or weakening a view of a founder's insight, integrity, adaptability, recruiting ability, speed, and founder-market fit; charisma and credentials alone are not evidence of founder quality.
_Avoid_: Founder vibes, pedigree score

**Sector Discovery Cycle**:
A comparative rotation through technology domains that measures sustained curiosity, access, analytical advantage, and emerging insight before the learner earns a focused thesis.
_Avoid_: Premature sector thesis, trend chasing

**Breadth Rotation**:
One completed sector week in the first phase of a Sector Discovery Cycle, containing five Snapshot Judgments and Forecasts, one Weekly Underwrite, and a dated comparison with previously visited sectors.
_Avoid_: Sector sampler, reading-only theme week

**Confirmation Sprint**:
Three completed weeks of deeper work in a finalist sector, adding repeated company judgments, Underwrites, external conversation evidence, a Second-Order Map, a provisional thesis, and a Countercase.
_Avoid_: Automatic specialization, extended content binge

**Provisional Focus**:
The evidence-qualified lead sector produced by a Sector Discovery Cycle. It receives roughly 70% of sector-specific practice while remaining revisable and exposed to runner-up and disconfirming evidence.
_Avoid_: Permanent identity, exclusive mandate, fashionable thesis

**Judgment Coach**:
The Lab's strict but diagnostic teaching role, which challenges unsupported claims, adapts practice to recurring weaknesses, compares current work with prior evidence, and requires revision when a gap is foundational.
_Avoid_: Cheerleader, answer generator, school-style grader

**Independent First Pass**:
The learner's committed analysis and confidence recorded before the Judgment Coach reveals competing interpretations, benchmarks, or a model analysis.
_Avoid_: Guided answer, imitation presented as judgment
