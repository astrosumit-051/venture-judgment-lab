# Sustainable Weekly Practice Architecture — 2026-08-05

This record defines how Venture Judgment Lab fits into a sustainable college schedule. It was accepted by the learner on August 5, 2026, then checked with a disposable schedule-state prototype before becoming part of the Private Learning Record.

## Operating rule

The Lab defaults to a Normal Week. A different mode replaces it only when its conditions apply. Modes change the mix of work rather than adding hours.

Priority when conditions overlap:

1. **Exam Mode** protects academic obligations.
2. **Recruiting Surge** protects a dated external opportunity.
3. **Monthly Calibration Week** performs the month's evidence review.
4. **Normal Week** is the default.

A missed or displaced exercise is recorded with its reason. It is not silently deleted, rewritten, or stacked onto a later week as catch-up debt.

## Normal Week — 690 minutes / 11.5 hours

### Five flexible weekday blocks — 450 minutes

Each weekday receives one 90-minute Daily Judgment Loop:

| Activity | Minutes |
| --- | ---: |
| Four-reading Daily Brief | Up to 55 |
| Snapshot Judgment on one early-stage company | 20 |
| Falsifiable Forecast | 10 |
| Preserve the dated record | 5 |
| **Daily total** | **90** |

The five weekday blocks total 450 minutes. The blocks are not tied to clock times; the later delivery-design work will decide reminders and notification behavior.

### One flexible weekend block — 240 minutes

| Activity | Minutes |
| --- | ---: |
| Weekly Underwrite | 165 |
| Recruiting | 60 |
| Reflection and next-week planning | 15 |
| **Weekend total** | **240** |

The Weekly Underwrite should normally use a company encountered during the week's Daily Judgment Loops so daily work compounds into the deep analysis.

## Monthly Calibration Week — 690 minutes / 11.5 hours

The five weekday Daily Judgment Loops remain unchanged at 450 minutes. The weekend block changes to:

| Activity | Minutes |
| --- | ---: |
| Weekly Underwrite | 120 |
| Calibration Review | 60 |
| Recruiting | 45 |
| Reflection and next-week planning | 15 |
| **Weekend total** | **240** |

The Calibration Review appends later evidence, resolution status, scoring, and reflection to earlier Forecasts and Snapshot Judgments. It never edits the original probability, reasoning, or timestamp.

Run this mode once per calendar month, normally in the last full week. If Exam Mode or Recruiting Surge takes priority, record the displacement; do not stack an extra review on the following week. The next Calibration Review may cover any still-unresolved earlier evidence.

## Recruiting Surge — 690 minutes / 11.5 hours

Activate Recruiting Surge when a dated application, interview, or relationship commitment cannot be completed within the Normal Week's one-hour recruiting block. Record the opportunity, deadline, reason for activation, and exercises displaced.

### Weekday allocation — 450 minutes

| Activity | Minutes |
| --- | ---: |
| Three Daily Judgment Loops | 270 |
| Deadline work replacing two Daily Judgment Loops | 180 |
| **Weekday total** | **450** |

### Weekend allocation — 240 minutes

| Activity | Minutes |
| --- | ---: |
| Compressed Weekly Underwrite | 90 |
| Deadline work replacing Underwrite time | 135 |
| Reflection and substitution record | 15 |
| **Weekend total** | **240** |

This exposes 315 minutes, or 5 hours 15 minutes, for recruiting while preserving three Independent First Pass repetitions and a compressed deep analysis.

An application company analysis or thesis may count as the Weekly Underwrite only when it contains:

- an Independent First Pass recorded before coaching;
- supporting and disconfirming evidence;
- a genuine Countercase; and
- Judgment Coach review.

If those conditions are absent, the Weekly Underwrite is marked displaced rather than falsely completed.

## Exam Mode — 180 minutes / 3 hours

The learner activates Exam Mode for an exam-heavy or unusually difficult academic week. Academic obligations take precedence even when another Lab mode was scheduled.

| Activity | Minutes |
| --- | ---: |
| One complete Daily Brief | 60 |
| One Snapshot Judgment and Forecast | 30 |
| Urgent recruiting or opportunity monitoring | 60 |
| Weekly reflection and restart plan | 30 |
| **Weekly total** | **180** |

When no recruiting obligation is urgent, redirect that hour to a 30-minute shortened Underwrite and 30 minutes of durable reading. Exam Mode creates no catch-up requirement. The next week begins in whichever mode its actual conditions require.

## Reading and interruption policies

- The Daily Brief always contains four curated selections, but it never exceeds its time cap.
- A long memo, paper, or report is serialized across days and remains one Reading Record entry with dated continuation notes.
- When a weekday is missed, preserve the scheduled activity, missed status, date, and reason. Do not double the next day's workload.
- Unavailable links remain in the Reading Record with their status; they are not silently replaced in historical entries.
- The Judgment Coach evaluates evidence, reasoning, calibration, and revision quality. It does not reward streaks, time spent, length, or completion alone.

## Four-week example

| Week | Default mode | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Normal Week | Establish the daily rhythm and first Weekly Underwrite. |
| 2 | Normal Week | Repeat the rhythm and compare fast judgment with deeper work. |
| 3 | Normal Week, or Recruiting Surge if triggered | Preserve practice unless a real dated opportunity requires substitution. |
| 4 | Monthly Calibration Week | Review prior evidence while retaining daily repetitions and a shorter Underwrite. |

Exam Mode may replace any week. Its activation and displaced work remain visible in the Private Learning Record.

## Prototype verdict

The disposable model verified:

- Normal Week: 690 minutes and five Daily Judgment Loops.
- Monthly Calibration Week: 690 minutes and five Daily Judgment Loops.
- Recruiting Surge: 690 minutes, three Daily Judgment Loops, and 315 recruiting minutes.
- Exam Mode: 180 minutes with either urgent recruiting or an Underwrite-and-reading fallback.
- Missed-day and long-reading scenarios add no hidden minutes or catch-up debt.

The prototype answered the scheduling question and was removed after this decision was preserved.
