# Venture Judgment Lab Sites Release — 2026-08-05

This record preserves the second private release of the Venture Judgment Lab
learning surface. The release corrects the learner-reviewed article-delivery
failure in version 1 without deleting or rewriting any earlier Lab record.

## Release decision

The private site now delivers one complete evidence-first learning loop:

- a 51-minute Daily Brief with four verified, lawfully accessible original
  sources and exact assigned sections;
- one immutable Reading Record per source, including source role, dates,
  access, rights, teaching purpose, learner response, and downstream use;
- a 20-minute Snapshot Judgment with linked support, visible disconfirmation,
  disposition, confidence, crux, unknown, and next evidence;
- a falsifiable Forecast with probability, resolution date, disconfirming
  condition, resolution source, and append-only resolution;
- a Second-Order Map across bottlenecks, incentives, suppliers, customers,
  substitutes, regulation, adjacent effects, and disconfirmation;
- a Weekly Underwrite linked to a locked Snapshot, exactly three Load-Bearing
  Questions, three claim-linked Evidence Ledger entries, material support and
  challenge, Founder Evidence or a precise gap, a Countercase, final causal
  case, Decision Delta, and next evidence;
- all four accepted weekly practice modes with enforced hour and repetition
  arithmetic; and
- a private History that permits only dated appended events after commitment.

Sites remains the interactive surface. The dated records in this workspace and
the private database are the evidence record; no raw learner work is public.
