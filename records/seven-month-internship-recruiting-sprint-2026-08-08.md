# Seven-Month Internship Recruiting Sprint

**Accepted:** 2026-08-08

**Operating window:** August 2026 through February 2027

**Canonical surface:** Private Learning Record; the Sites workspace is a private interaction layer

**Outcome:** Produce evidence-backed access to a paid Summer 2027 Qualifying Internship while continuing the Venture Judgment Lab inside the existing 10–12-hour weekly budget.

## Operating standard

Recruiting and judgment development run concurrently. The sprint measures distinct Qualified roles, referrals, confirmed applications, completed interviews, and offers. It does not score firms, award points for activity, or count Investing Milestones as paid internships.

Every opportunity begins as an immutable Recruiting Opportunity. Later facts become immutable child records:

1. Opportunity Observation — material first-party or privacy-bounded status evidence.
2. Recruiting Interaction — behavioral relationship, referral, inquiry, follow-up, or interview evidence without raw messages or contact details.
3. Application Attempt — artifacts, claim ledger, authorization wording, explicit approval, and confirmation evidence.
4. Interview Practice — Independent First Pass, evidence, unsupported claim or gap, and revision.
5. Portfolio Candidate — a private pointer to learner-owned Lab work with ownership, confidentiality, redaction, and recruiting-use review.

One normalized first-party URL identifies a Recruiting Opportunity for one owner. One deterministic record key identifies each child within its parent. Child evidence must preserve the parent's timezone, cannot predate discovery, and cannot attach to another record type.

An Opportunity Observation may preserve an evidence-backed reclassification while the original class remains visible in history. The effective class and its funnel class must agree. Archiving changes active status but does not erase that role's already evidenced referral, application, interview, or offer conversions.

### November — calibrate the funnel

- Compare qualified-role denominators with referrals, confirmed submissions, interviews, and offers.
- Diagnose the constrained transition using evidence, not application-volume intuition.

### December — deepen relationships and fallbacks

### January 2027 — interview and authorization readiness

### February 2027 — decision and recovery paths

- Compare offers on role scope, paid status, access to investment work, timing, and documented immigration compatibility.
- Treat only a paid direct-investing internship as a Qualifying Internship.
- If the primary funnel has not converted, activate fallbacks without erasing the original target evidence or inflating adjacent outcomes.

## Immigration evidence transitions

The allowed progression is evidence-based, not automatic:

`Unknown → General eligibility → DSO-confirmed role fit → Employer-compatible → Authorized`

- Unknown: no preserved role-specific evidence.
- General eligibility: a general institutional or program rule exists, but the exact role is unconfirmed.
- DSO-confirmed role fit: Centre's DSO has assessed the actual role facts.
- Employer-compatible: the employer's timing, location, documentation, and hiring constraints are compatible with the assessed path.
- Authorized: the required role-specific authorization is documented. Only this state may support an authorization claim.

An opportunity may remain at, or return to, a lower effective evidence state when new facts invalidate an earlier assumption. Original evidence remains unchanged.

## External-action and privacy gates

- Research and drafting are allowed locally.
- Sending outreach, referral requests, or DSO correspondence requires explicit learner approval.
- Claiming an application was submitted requires explicit learner approval and preserved confirmation evidence.
- A Portfolio Candidate remains private. “Approved for recruiting use” requires explicit learner approval plus a cleared confidentiality review and still does not publish it.
- Raw correspondence, contact details, transcripts, private attachments, prestige scores, and undeclared fields are rejected from recruiting records.
- No immigration statement may imply role authorization unless the evidence state is Authorized.

## Time substitutions

The sprint never creates a second workload above the Lab:

- Normal Week: use the existing 60 recruiting minutes plus the flexible weekend block.
- Recruiting Surge: 315 recruiting minutes replace two Daily Loops and part of the Underwrite while the week remains 690 minutes.
- Exam Mode: urgent recruiting can use 60 of the 180 protected minutes; there is no catch-up debt.
- A 10–15-hour Investing Milestone, including Dorm Room Fund, replaces the week's Lab workload. It does not stack on top.

## Fallback logic

1. Direct paid early-stage investment internship.
2. Paid accelerator or emerging-manager investment role that meets the direct-work definition.
3. Investing Milestone with real decision ownership, counted separately and substituted for Lab time.
4. Paid startup, consulting, or finance internship that develops useful evidence but is explicitly a Fallback.
5. Continue the Lab and relationship-led search without relabeling a non-qualifying outcome.

The sprint succeeds through auditable judgment, truthful claims, and conversion evidence—not through the number or prestige of targets entered.
