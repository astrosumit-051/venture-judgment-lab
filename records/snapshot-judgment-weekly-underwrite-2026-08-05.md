# Snapshot Judgment and Weekly Underwrite Standard — 2026-08-05

This record defines how Venture Judgment Lab trains rapid pattern recognition and deep early-stage diligence. The learner approved the model on August 5, 2026 after reviewing a disposable state prototype across narrative-heavy, sparse-evidence, and contradictory-evidence company cases.

## Operating principle

The Snapshot Judgment and Weekly Underwrite are two different judgments about the same company, separated by evidence and time.

- The Snapshot commits a useful but incomplete Independent First Pass within 20 minutes.
- The Underwrite investigates the three claims most capable of changing that view.
- The original Snapshot remains immutable. The Underwrite appends a deeper disposition, confidence, and explanation of what changed.
- Required evidence safeguards replace comprehensive category completion. The Lab never produces a weighted startup score.

## Snapshot Judgment — 20 minutes

The learner normally selects a company encountered through the week's Daily Briefs or independent sourcing. The 20-minute cap is a stopping rule: unanswered questions remain visible instead of extending the exercise until the analysis looks complete.

### Committed fields

Before submission, record:

- company, stage, sector, source of discovery, date, time, and timezone;
- a one-sentence investment thesis written as a causal claim;
- the mechanism by which the company could create venture-scale value;
- a **Practice Disposition** of Pursue, Watch, or Pass;
- confidence from 0 to 100 percent;
- the single crux on which the disposition most depends;
- the strongest available supporting evidence, linked to its source;
- the strongest available disconfirming signal, or an explicit statement that none was found in the timebox;
- the most important unresolved uncertainty; and
- the next observable evidence that would materially change the view.

The Snapshot is valid when it commits a reasoned view under uncertainty. It is not invalid merely because public evidence is sparse. Unsupported certainty, invented evidence, hidden uncertainty, or a disposition without a causal explanation makes it invalid.

### Practice Dispositions

| Disposition | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pursue** | Current evidence justifies spending scarce diligence or relationship time now. |
| **Watch** | A named uncertainty or observable trigger should govern the next look. |
| **Pass** | Current evidence does not justify more Lab time unless a stated condition changes. |

These are practice-allocation decisions, not simulated investment-committee votes. A Watch is not a safe default: it requires a precise trigger. A Pass is not a claim that the company will fail.

## Selecting the Weekly Underwrite company

Normally choose one of the week's five Snapshot Judgments using this order:

1. the company with a load-bearing uncertainty that can be investigated with available evidence;
2. the company whose deeper analysis would most test a recurring learner weakness;
3. the company most relevant to the active Sector Discovery Cycle or a genuine recruiting deadline; then
4. the company with the highest initial confidence, only when the earlier criteria do not distinguish candidates.

This prevents the learner from Underwriting only the most exciting or easiest company. The selection reason is recorded.

## Weekly Underwrite

### 1. Reopen without rewriting

Display the original Snapshot, its timestamp, disposition, confidence, crux, evidence, and unknowns. The learner may append a pre-diligence note but cannot edit the original judgment.

### 2. Frame three Load-Bearing Questions

A **Load-Bearing Question** asks about a claim whose answer could materially change the Practice Disposition or confidence. Select exactly three for the Underwrite.

Possible domains include customer pain and behavior, product or technical differentiation, distribution, competitive response, market timing, unit economics, Founder Evidence, regulation, or financing risk. These are a question bank, not mandatory report sections. The company's actual causal mechanism determines which domains matter.

At least one question must challenge the venture-scale mechanism or the Snapshot's crux. Founder Evidence remains a completion invariant even when it is not one of the three questions; an explicit evidence gap is acceptable when observable behavior is unavailable.

### 3. Build the Evidence Ledger

Evidence is stored by claim rather than collected as a pile of links. Each entry records:

- the Load-Bearing Question and claim it informs;
- the observation, kept separate from the learner's inference;
- source identity, link, source role, publication or observation date, and access date;
- whether it supports, challenges, or complicates the claim;
- reliability limits, incentives, and missing context; and
- the learner's resulting inference and confidence.

The ledger must contain material supporting evidence, material disconfirming evidence, and Founder Evidence or a precise statement of the evidence gap. Repeated coverage of the same underlying claim does not create independent corroboration.

### 4. Write the Countercase

State the strongest evidence-based causal path by which the company fails to create venture-scale value or becomes a poor use of further diligence time. A generic risks list, imagined objection, or symmetrical paragraph written only for form does not qualify.

### 5. Commit the deeper judgment

Record:

- final Practice Disposition and confidence;
- the causal investment case;
- the strongest evidence for and against it;
- the Countercase;
- the **Decision Delta**: what was retained, changed, or reversed from the Snapshot and which evidence caused the update;
- the next decisive evidence and how it could change the view; and
- links to related Forecasts, Reading Records, sector work, conversations, or recruiting artifacts.

The Decision Delta may legitimately be "no change," but it must explain why the deeper evidence did not move the view. Confidence movement without a reason is not accepted.

### 6. Coach after commitment

The Judgment Coach does not reveal its interpretation, benchmarks, or model analysis before the learner commits the Underwrite. Feedback is appended afterward and cannot alter the original Snapshot or Underwrite. Revision becomes a new dated response.

## Time architecture

The same judgment invariants apply in each permitted weekly mode. Compression changes evidence depth, not truthfulness or completion standards.

| Underwrite mode | Reopen and frame | Evidence sprints | Countercase | Synthesis and Decision Delta | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Normal Week | 10 | 105 | 20 | 30 | **165 min** |
| Monthly Calibration Week | 10 | 70 | 15 | 25 | **120 min** |
| Recruiting Surge | 10 | 50 | 10 | 20 | **90 min** |

Evidence sprints follow the three Load-Bearing Questions. No generic section receives mandatory minutes. When the timebox expires, unresolved evidence is recorded and becomes part of the final confidence and next-evidence statement.

An application company analysis may count as the Underwrite only when it preserves the Snapshot, questions, Evidence Ledger, disconfirmation, Founder Evidence or gap, Countercase, Decision Delta, and post-commitment coach review. Otherwise it is recruiting work, not a completed Underwrite.

## Completion invariants

A Weekly Underwrite counts only when all of the following exist:

1. the immutable, timestamped Snapshot Judgment;
2. a recorded reason for choosing the company;
3. exactly three company-specific Load-Bearing Questions;
4. a traceable Evidence Ledger containing support and disconfirmation;
5. Founder Evidence or an explicit evidence gap;
6. a genuine Countercase grounded in evidence;
7. final Practice Disposition and confidence;
8. a Decision Delta tied to specific evidence;
9. the next decisive evidence; and
10. a committed timestamp before Judgment Coach interpretation.

These are integrity constraints, not graded boxes. Completion earns no quality credit by itself. The later coaching standard will evaluate evidence, causal reasoning, calibration, updating, and communication without collapsing them into one company score.

## Edge-case decisions

- **Sparse public evidence:** Watch or Pass with low confidence and explicit unknowns is valid; invented completeness is not.
- **Persuasive founder narrative:** first-party statements count as evidence of what was claimed, not independent proof of adoption or performance.
- **Contradictory evidence:** preserve both observations, explain their different source roles, and let the controlling uncertainty govern the disposition.
- **No disconfirming evidence found:** the Snapshot may record that result because it is time-boxed; the Weekly Underwrite cannot complete until it finds material contrary evidence or documents why the evidence environment itself prevents a responsible disposition.
- **Missing Founder Evidence:** record the gap and the behavior that would resolve it; never substitute pedigree, charisma, or inferred personality.
- **No decision change:** retain the original disposition only with an evidence-based Decision Delta explaining why the deeper work reinforced it.
- **Coach disagreement:** append the critique and learner response. Do not replace the committed judgment with the coach's view.

## Private Learning Record and Sites implications

- The Snapshot and Underwrite are separate immutable submissions connected by a Decision Delta.
- Evidence entries, corrections, unavailable links, later outcomes, coach feedback, and revisions are dated append-only events.
- Sites should show the locked Snapshot beside the active Underwrite so updating is visible rather than reconstructed from memory.
- The Underwrite workspace should foreground three Load-Bearing Questions and the Evidence Ledger, not a dashboard of category scores.
- Coach interpretation remains inaccessible until commitment.
- The complete record stays private unless a separately sanitized Public Portfolio Artifact is approved.

## Prototype verdict

The disposable state prototype verified that:

- deep diligence cannot begin before the Snapshot is locked;
- the original Snapshot remains unchanged after deeper evidence appears;
- supporting evidence alone cannot complete an Underwrite;
- a Countercase cannot be written before material disconfirming evidence exists;
- Founder Evidence or an explicit gap remains visible;
- the final disposition requires a Decision Delta and next decisive evidence; and
- coach critique appears only after learner commitment.

The learner approved this model on August 5, 2026. The prototype's answer was absorbed into this record and the disposable implementation was removed.
