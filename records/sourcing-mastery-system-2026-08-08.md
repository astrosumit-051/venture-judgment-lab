# Sourcing Mastery System — 2026-08-08

This record defines how Venture Judgment Lab turns the learner's prior Awesome
Fund screening and outreach exposure into auditable, independently attributable
sourcing judgment. It rewards neither list volume nor persuasive retrospective
stories. Quality appears when a dated discovery produces better questions,
founder access, a Snapshot Judgment, a Weekly Underwrite, or later evidence.

## Three linked records

### Sourcing Experiment

A Sourcing Experiment is a bounded, falsifiable test of one discovery method.
It commits the channel, target segment, exact search surface, discovery
hypothesis, leading signal, non-consensus rationale, date window, planned lead
count, success condition, and stop-or-change rule before the result is known.

An experiment is not successful because it produced many tabs or company
names. Its result is the funnel created by explicitly linked Sourcing Leads and
their later Snapshots, conversations, relationships, and Underwrites.

### Sourcing Lead

A Sourcing Lead is the immutable first record of one company discovery. It
preserves:

- company identity and website;
- original discovery date, channel, source, and Sourcing Attribution Class;
- the server-recorded discovery timestamp, which follows the linked experiment
  commit and matches the local discovery date;
- the observed signal and why it may be early or overlooked;
- a fast qualification thesis and venture-scale mechanism;
- the strongest disqualifier or exact evidence gap;
- the initial Sourcing Disposition;
- an evidence-specific founder outreach angle; and
- one next action with a due date.

One company domain has one Sourcing Lead. Rediscovery through another channel,
a changed signal, or a later relationship is appended to that record rather
than counted as another company.

### Sourcing Progress

Sourcing Progress is an append-only event attached to a Sourcing Lead. It
preserves the evidence date, reached funnel stage, outreach medium, observed
interaction evidence, relationship-quality state, outcome, and next action.
It never overwrites the original signal or attribution.

## Sourcing Attribution Classes

Attribution describes how the learner actually encountered the company:

- **Independent discovery:** the learner chose the search surface and surfaced
  the company through original public, technical, customer, community, or
  relationship investigation—not from an institutional company list,
  database result set, or assigned company.
- **Database screening:** the company came from PitchBook or another
  institutional company database, even when the learner designed the query.
- **Assigned search:** a fund, course, mentor, or employer defined the company
  or list to screen.
- **Referral or inbound:** another person or organization introduced or sent
  the opportunity.
- **Existing relationship:** the opportunity originated from a relationship
  already held by the learner.

All five can produce valuable work. Only the first counts as independent
discovery. A later independent insight does not rewrite an assigned or
database-sourced origin.

## Funnel stages

Progress advances at most one stage per append:

1. Discovered.
2. Qualified.
3. Outreach sent.
4. Response received.
5. Founder meeting.
6. Relationship active.

Same-stage updates are valid when they add evidence. Stages never skip or move
backward. Corrections use a dated metadata-correction path with a typed
corrected value rather than editing history. The latest valid correction becomes
the effective value used by current display, cohort reporting, and downstream
identity checks while the original value remains visible in the immutable lead.
Discovery-provenance corrections atomically restate the attribution class,
channel, visibility, and source reference so a partial correction cannot create
a contradictory origin. Reaching a stage requires observed evidence; sending a
message is not a response, and receiving a polite reply is not automatically a
relationship.

## Relationship evidence

Relationship quality uses observable states rather than a relationship score:

- no direct interaction;
- one-way contact;
- responsive exchange;
- reciprocal learning; or
- trusted relationship.

The learner records the behavior supporting the state. Warmth, prestige,
contact count, or a founder's willingness to accept a meeting cannot substitute
for reciprocal evidence.

## Downstream quality and conversion

A qualified Sourcing Lead may become the explicit parent of a Snapshot
Judgment. Company identity is locked across the link. A Weekly Underwrite then
links to that Snapshot under the existing exact-parent rule. This produces an
auditable path from discovery to deeper judgment without claiming that a
Snapshot, Underwrite, or investment outcome proves the original source was
good.

The Lab reports:

- qualification rate: qualified leads divided by discovered leads;
- reply rate: responses divided by outreach sent;
- meeting rate: founder meetings divided by outreach sent;
- Snapshot and Underwrite conversion per discovered lead; and
- the same downstream counts by Attribution Class and discovery channel.

Small samples remain visible as small samples. An investment outcome is later
evidence, not a retroactive proof of sourcing skill. Monthly Calibration Reviews
interpret the funnel, compare channels, diagnose false positives and missed
signals, and change one future sourcing rule.

## Privacy and outreach integrity

Public discoveries preserve the original source URL. Private relationships and
internal assignments preserve only a concise, consented context summary. Raw
messages, contact details, transcripts, confidential information, founder
ratings, and undeclared fields are rejected from initial and progress records.
Every progress update requires an explicit privacy confirmation.

The Lab preserves an outreach angle and observed outcome, not a mass-message
script or private correspondence archive. Founder contact must remain specific,
respectful, and truthful about the learner's role.

## Weekly integration

The system adds no hours. A Sourcing Experiment is set during weekly planning.
Its strongest leads supply companies for the existing Daily Judgment Loops and
Weekly Underwrite. Outreach and follow-up use the existing recruiting block.
Recruiting Surge and Exam Mode substitute work under their approved rules; they
do not create a sourcing backlog.

The Awesome Fund funnel remains dated baseline evidence. It is not backfilled
as newly independent Lab sourcing unless company-level provenance and ownership
can be defended.

## Completion rules

- The experiment hypothesis and success rule are committed before results.
- Every lead has one normalized company identity and an honest Attribution
  Class.
- Private context contains no raw communications or contact details.
- Sourcing Progress is dated, privacy-confirmed, and advances no more than one
  stage at a time.
- A linked Snapshot matches the Sourcing Lead's latest effective company and
  provenance while preserving its original values and correction events.
- Funnel reporting includes denominators and downstream evidence.
- No company, relationship, channel, or learner mastery score is calculated.
