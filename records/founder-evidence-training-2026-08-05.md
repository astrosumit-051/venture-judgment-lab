# Founder Evidence Training Standard — 2026-08-05

This record defines how Venture Judgment Lab trains founder judgment through
observable behavior rather than charisma, pedigree, status, or hindsight. A
Founder Evidence Review is one dated interpretation of one evidence encounter;
it is not a permanent founder grade.

## Evidence encounters

A review may use:

- a public founder interview or talk;
- a direct founder conversation;
- a reference conversation; or
- a concrete customer or employee observation.

Public material must link to the original source. Private interactions record
the context and behavioral evidence without preserving raw transcripts or
information that was not consented for reuse. Every review names source
limitations, incentives, editing, selection effects, and its privacy boundary.

## Six behavior dimensions

Every review examines the same six dimensions, but it never combines them into
a weighted score:

1. **Insight:** specific, earned understanding of the problem, customer, or
   system.
2. **Speed:** fast learning, execution, or decision-making without reckless
   shortcuts.
3. **Integrity:** behavior around bad news, uncertainty, incentives,
   attribution, and commitments.
4. **Recruiting ability:** observable evidence of attracting and retaining
   unusually capable people.
5. **Adaptability:** proportionate updating after contradictory evidence.
6. **Founder-market fit:** earned access, lived experience, technical depth, or
   durable motivation specific to the market.

For each dimension, the learner records a direction—supports, weakens, or
evidence gap—the observed behavior, and the limited inference it supports. A
gap is valid only when it names the behavior that could not be observed.

## Halo-effect and counterevidence discipline

The learner explicitly removes impressions created by confidence, polish,
school, employer, funder, network status, or storytelling ability. The review
then preserves the strongest counterevidence or the gap that most limits the
emerging view.

The final judgment is provisional and carries confidence only in the
evidence-based view, not in a founder's essential quality. It ends with one
next decisive question and one behavioral prediction that later evidence can
strengthen or weaken.

## Weekly Underwrite integration

A Founder Evidence Review may link to a locked Snapshot Judgment. A Weekly
Underwrite may link only to a review explicitly attached to that same Snapshot.
Standalone reviews remain useful practice records but are not
Underwrite-eligible. The Underwrite still summarizes the material Founder Evidence
or explicitly records the remaining gap; linking a review does not replace
synthesis.

Later founder behavior, references, and coach feedback append to the original
review or become a new dated review. Appended updates use the same privacy
confirmation and reject transcripts, ratings, and undeclared material. Earlier
interpretations are never edited to match later outcomes.

## Completion rules

- All six dimensions appear exactly once.
- Observation and inference remain separate in every dimension.
- Public interviews have an original source link.
- Observation dates cannot be in the future.
- Source limitations, privacy boundary, halo-effect check, and counterevidence
  are required.
- A private-source review explicitly confirms that only consented behavioral
  evidence is preserved and that raw transcripts and confidential details are
  omitted.
- No overall founder score is stored or calculated.
- A review linked into an Underwrite must be explicitly attached to that exact
  Snapshot.
