# Sector Discovery Cycle — 2026-08-05

This record defines the first approved Sector Discovery Cycle for Venture Judgment Lab. The learner approved the twelve-week model on August 5, 2026, after reviewing a disposable comparison and graduation prototype.

## Purpose

The cycle must explore technology broadly enough to prevent a fashionable or premature thesis while reaching a useful Provisional Focus quickly enough to support live recruiting. It uses the existing weekly practice architecture; it adds no hours.

## Twelve-week funnel

### Weeks 1–6 — Breadth Rotations

Spend one completed rotation in each contrasting domain, in this order:

1. Enterprise software and vertical SaaS.
2. Industrial, climate, and physical systems.
3. Fintech and financial infrastructure.
4. Digital health and bio tools.
5. AI and data infrastructure.
6. Cybersecurity and digital trust.

Each rotation uses one Normal or Monthly Calibration Week and contains:

- five Daily Briefs grounded partly in the sector while retaining cross-domain and career lanes;
- five Snapshot Judgments and Forecasts;
- one Weekly Underwrite; and
- one dated comparison against the sectors already visited.

The order alternates familiar and contrasting environments so existing B2B SaaS exposure, Economics and Data Science training, or current AI excitement cannot silently determine the result.

### Weeks 7–12 — Confirmation Sprints

Advance the two strongest breadth sectors into three-week Confirmation Sprints. Each sector's sprint adds:

- fifteen Snapshot Judgments and Forecasts;
- three Weekly Underwrites;
- at least one relevant founder, operator, customer, or investor conversation;
- one Second-Order Map;
- one falsifiable provisional thesis; and
- the strongest evidence-based Countercase.

Across breadth and confirmation, each finalist therefore accumulates at least twenty Snapshot Judgments and four Weekly Underwrites.

## Comparative evidence

Use evidence states rather than school-style grades or a weighted popularity score:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 — none | No evidence exists yet. |
| 1 — asserted | The learner states a preference or advantage but has not demonstrated it. |
| 2 — demonstrated once | One concrete artifact or interaction supports it. |
| 3 — repeated or corroborated | Multiple artifacts or independent evidence support it. |

Append a dated evidence state and its supporting artifact to five dimensions after each rotation or sprint week:

1. **Sustained curiosity:** better questions continue after easy novelty disappears.
2. **Reachable access:** relevant founders, customers, operators, or investors can be reached and produce useful learning.
3. **Analytical advantage:** Economics, Data Science, lived experience, or accumulated knowledge produces sharper analysis.
4. **Original insight:** the learner identifies a specific causal mechanism or non-obvious view rather than repeating a market narrative.
5. **Quality deal flow:** the learner independently finds credible early-stage companies in the domain.

Evidence states may rise or fall through dated updates. Earlier states and their supporting rationale remain unchanged.

## Selecting the two Confirmation Sprints

Do not advance sectors until all six Breadth Rotations are complete. Compare sectors in this order:

1. Number of dimensions supported by repeated or corroborated evidence.
2. Number of remaining dimensions demonstrated at least once.
3. Strength and independence of supporting artifacts and external evidence.
4. Quality of independently discovered early-stage companies.

If the second position remains tied, prefer the sector that creates the sharper contrast with the leading sector across customer, business model, technical risk, or regulatory environment. The purpose is to test where the learner's advantage survives different conditions, not to create a false numerical ranking.

## Graduation into a Provisional Focus

A finalist may become the Provisional Focus only when:

- all six Breadth Rotations are complete;
- its three-week Confirmation Sprint is complete;
- at least twenty Snapshot Judgments and four Weekly Underwrites exist;
- at least one relevant external conversation and one Second-Order Map exist;
- a falsifiable provisional thesis and genuine Countercase exist;
- every comparison dimension has been demonstrated at least once; and
- at least two dimensions have repeated or corroborated evidence.

When both finalists qualify, apply the same comparison order used to select the finalists. The leader becomes the Provisional Focus; the other remains the runner-up.

If neither finalist qualifies, do not force a winner. Continue one targeted Confirmation Sprint at a time, aimed at the unresolved evidence gaps, until one sector qualifies or evidence justifies replacing a finalist.

After graduation, allocate roughly 70% of sector-specific practice to the Provisional Focus and 30% to the runner-up, adjacent domains, and deliberate disconfirmation. The Provisional Focus remains revisable; it is not a permanent identity.

## Interaction with weekly modes

- A Breadth Rotation counts as complete only when its five Snapshot Judgments and Forecasts, Weekly Underwrite, and comparison note exist.
- A Confirmation Sprint week counts only when its planned sector artifacts exist.
- Monthly Calibration Week may count because it preserves five Daily Judgment Loops and a Weekly Underwrite.
- When Recruiting Surge or Exam Mode prevents the required artifacts, mark the sector week interrupted and resume that sector in the next eligible week. Do not add catch-up hours or pretend the rotation completed.
- An application analysis may count as a sector Underwrite only when it satisfies the existing Independent First Pass, evidence, Countercase, and Judgment Coach requirements.

The cycle is therefore twelve completed sector weeks, not an inflexible twelve-calendar-week streak.

## Prototype verdict

The disposable logic prototype verified that:

- high curiosity alone cannot earn a focus;
- even a fully evidenced sector cannot graduate before all six Breadth Rotations;
- the complete path requires repeated artifacts, external evidence, a Second-Order Map, a thesis, and a Countercase; and
- the cycle fits inside the approved weekly practice modes without adding hours.

The learner approved the prototype as written. Its answer was absorbed here and the prototype was removed.
