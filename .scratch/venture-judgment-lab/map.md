# Build and operate Venture Judgment Lab

Label: wayfinder:map

## Destination

A private, dynamic, evidence-preserving apprenticeship is operating through Sites and the local learning record: it makes the learner competitive for a qualifying Summer 2027 early-stage technology investing internship by the Internship Readiness Gate, then continues adapting until the learner reaches the Destination Event.

## Notes

- This effort explicitly carries execution into the map: resolving tickets may create curriculum artifacts, daily briefs, assessments, automations, and working Sites functionality, not merely a handoff specification.
- Consult `CONTEXT.md`, relevant records in `docs/adr/`, and the `wayfinder` and `domain-modeling` skills every session. Use `sites-building` and `sites-hosting` for site work and current primary sources for external research.
- Preserve the learner's Independent First Pass, dates, confidence, later updates, and errors. Never rewrite historical judgment with hindsight.
- The Private Learning Record is canonical. Sites is the interactive surface. Public Portfolio Artifacts require deliberate sanitation and approval.
- The sustainable practice budget is 10–12 hours per week. The Daily Brief has four balanced readings with a 55-minute total cap.
- Teaching is strict and diagnostic. Do not substitute checklists, completion praise, or model answers for learner judgment.
- Summer 2027 recruiting is live now. Development and applications proceed concurrently from August 5, 2026; never restore March 2027 as a general readiness date.

## Decisions so far

- [Research the Summer 2027 early-stage investing internship market](issues/01-research-summer-2027-internship-market.md) — Recruiting is already live: pursue Bessemer now, treat Pear and Dorm Room Fund as urgent Investing Milestones, and screen every target for CPT and later STEM OPT compatibility.
- [Establish the learner evidence baseline](issues/02-establish-learner-evidence-baseline.md) — Build from real sourcing-funnel and founder-facing experience while closing attribution, independent sourcing, diligence, writing, technical-proof, judgment-track-record, and visa-precision gaps.
- [Design the sustainable weekly practice architecture](issues/03-design-sustainable-practice-architecture.md) — Use four explicit modes: 11.5-hour Normal and Monthly weeks, a substitution-based Recruiting Surge, and a three-hour Exam Mode with no hidden backlog.
- [Design the Sector Discovery Cycle](issues/04-design-sector-discovery-cycle.md) — Compare six one-week sector rotations, confirm the strongest two for three weeks each, and award only an evidence-qualified, revisable 70/30 Provisional Focus.
- [Define the Daily Brief curation and Reading Record standard](issues/05-define-daily-brief-standard.md) — Curate four distinct readings within 55 minutes through evidence, purpose, access, freshness, balance, deduplication, rights, and preservation gates, then preserve each assignment and every later event without rewriting history.
- [Design the rapid screen and Weekly Underwrite](issues/06-design-rapid-screen-and-weekly-underwrite.md) — Lock a causal 20-minute Snapshot, then investigate three Load-Bearing Questions through traceable support, disconfirmation, Founder Evidence, a Countercase, and an explicit Decision Delta without producing a checklist score.
- [Design Forecast and Second-Order Map practice](issues/07-design-forecasting-and-second-order-practice.md) — Preserve immutable probabilistic Forecasts, trace explicit first-, second-, and third-order consequences, and use atomic Monthly Calibration Reviews to resolve outcomes, calculate Brier scores, diagnose mistakes, and change future decision rules.

## Out of scope

- Training for later-stage private equity, control buyouts, or public-equity investing except where comparison improves early-stage judgment.
- Automatically publishing raw analyses, confidential company information, or internship-derived material.
- Claiming certainty, eliminating misses, or optimizing for a perfect investment record; the Lab develops calibrated positive expected judgment over time.
