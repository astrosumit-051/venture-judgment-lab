How should a Snapshot Judgment and an end-to-end early-stage company analysis train fast pattern recognition and deep diligence without collapsing into a mechanical checklist?

## Answer

Use the dated [`Snapshot Judgment and Weekly Underwrite Standard`](../../../records/snapshot-judgment-weekly-underwrite-2026-08-05.md). The learner commits a causal but incomplete 20-minute Snapshot before deep research. The Underwrite preserves that Snapshot, investigates exactly three company-specific Load-Bearing Questions through a traceable Evidence Ledger, requires supporting and disconfirming evidence, Founder Evidence or an explicit gap, and a genuine Countercase, then appends a final Practice Disposition, confidence, Decision Delta, and next decisive evidence before coach interpretation. The model uses integrity constraints rather than generic category coverage or a weighted startup score.
