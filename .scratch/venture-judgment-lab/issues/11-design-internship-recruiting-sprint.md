## Answer

Run recruiting and Lab development concurrently from August 2026 through February 2027 inside the existing weekly time budget. Begin every target as one immutable Recruiting Opportunity, then preserve changes through typed Opportunity Observations, Recruiting Interactions, Application Attempts, Interview Practice, and private Portfolio Candidates. First-party evidence can reclassify a target without rewriting its original class; archiving changes active status without erasing real prior funnel conversions.

Measure distinct evidence-backed Qualifying Internships through referrals, confirmed applications, completed interviews, and offers. Keep Investing Milestones, relationship targets, fallbacks, prestige, and activity volume outside that denominator. General eligibility never becomes role authorization by implication: preserve the exact progression from Unknown through Authorized, compare Application Attempts with the opportunity's effective evidence state, and allow an authorization claim only at Authorized.

All outreach, submission, and recruiting-use claims require explicit learner approval and the appropriate confirmation or confidentiality evidence. Raw correspondence, contact details, private attachments, and automatic publication remain outside the record. Use Normal Week, Recruiting Surge, Exam Mode, or full substitution for a 10–15-hour milestone so recruiting never creates hidden hours or catch-up debt. The canonical operating sequence, verified target baseline, and fallback logic are preserved in `records/seven-month-internship-recruiting-sprint-2026-08-08.md`; the private Sites workspace exposes the same contract without taking external action.
