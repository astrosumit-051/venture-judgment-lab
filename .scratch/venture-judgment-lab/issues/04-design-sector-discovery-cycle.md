## Answer

The dated [Sector Discovery Cycle](../../../records/sector-discovery-cycle-2026-08-05.md) establishes a twelve-week funnel: six contrasting one-week Breadth Rotations, followed by three-week Confirmation Sprints in each of the two strongest sectors. Sectors are compared through dated evidence of sustained curiosity, reachable access, analytical advantage, original insight, and quality deal flow rather than a weighted popularity score.

A Provisional Focus requires comparative breadth, at least twenty Snapshot Judgments, four Weekly Underwrites, external conversation evidence, a Second-Order Map, a falsifiable thesis, a genuine Countercase, demonstrated evidence across every dimension, and repeated or corroborated evidence in at least two. No winner is forced when the threshold is unmet, and interrupted weeks never create hidden catch-up hours or false completion.
