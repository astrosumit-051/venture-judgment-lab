# Design the sustainable weekly practice architecture

Type: prototype
Status: resolved
Parent: ../map.md

## Question

How should the agreed Daily Judgment Loop, Weekly Underwrite, monthly Calibration Review, recruiting work, exam fallback, and one-hour Daily Brief fit inside a genuinely sustainable 10–12-hour week?

## Comments

### 2026-08-05 — learner approved the four-mode schedule

The learner selected a daily rhythm, recruiting that replaces practice during genuine deadlines, and a three-hour Exam Mode floor. The approved implementation plan specified Normal Week, Monthly Calibration Week, Recruiting Surge, and Exam Mode.

### 2026-08-05 — disposable schedule prototype validated and absorbed

A temporary interactive schedule-state model exposed all four modes plus missed-day, long-reading, and urgent-recruiting scenarios. Normal and Monthly weeks each totaled 690 minutes; Recruiting Surge totaled 690 minutes with three Daily Judgment Loops and 315 recruiting minutes; Exam Mode totaled 180 minutes. Every scenario passed the approved constraints. The prototype was removed after its answer was preserved.

## Answer

The dated [Sustainable Weekly Practice Architecture](../../../records/sustainable-weekly-practice-architecture-2026-08-05.md) defines the Lab's four operating modes, substitution priority, exact time budgets, no-backlog behavior, reading serialization, and a four-week example. Normal and Monthly weeks remain at 11.5 hours, Recruiting Surge creates more than five hours for dated opportunities without exceeding the ceiling, and Exam Mode protects academic obligations with a three-hour continuity floor.

This decision supplies the time architecture for the Daily Brief, rapid-screen, Forecast, Weekly Underwrite, sourcing, recruiting, coaching, delivery, and Sites-experience milestones without prematurely specifying their internal teaching methods.
