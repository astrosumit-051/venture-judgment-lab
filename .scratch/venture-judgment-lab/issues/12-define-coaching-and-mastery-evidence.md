## Answer

Use the dated [Judgment Coach and Mastery Evidence Standard](../../../records/judgment-coach-and-mastery-evidence-2026-08-08.md). A Coach Request can target only one eligible dimension of already committed, company-linked Independent First Pass work. The secure Codex operator sees only queued requests and an explicit bounded source projection, then appends one diagnostic Coach Feedback record naming the unsupported inference, evidence gap, recurring error and occurrence count, required revision, competing interpretation, benchmark, Foundational Error state, and next difficulty adjustment. Grades, scores, model answers, undeclared private data, and generic History mutation are rejected.

One Revision Attempt preserves the learner's evidence-based response beside the original and feedback. Every feedback atomically appends dimension-specific Mastery Evidence. `repeated_or_corroborated` requires three distinct Independent First Pass attempts across at least two companies, a genuine revision or disconfirming case, and no Foundational Error in the latest two attempts; otherwise the state remains `observed_once` or `developing` with explicit remaining gaps. No composite score exists.

The private Lab now has a Coach workspace, bearer-protected queue and feedback endpoint, owner/parent/duplicate/privacy contracts, deterministic repeated-error and mastery evaluation, generated D1 migration, inspectable History evidence, and a synthetic round trip that reaches the approved threshold without rewriting any prior record.
