## Answer

Use the dated [Founder Evidence Training Standard](../../../records/founder-evidence-training-2026-08-05.md). Train on public interviews, direct conversations, references, and concrete customer or employee observations. For insight, speed, integrity, recruiting ability, adaptability, and founder-market fit, preserve direction, observed behavior, and limited inference without aggregation. Require source limitations, privacy boundaries, a charisma and pedigree check, counterevidence, a provisional confidence, the next decisive question, and a behavioral prediction. Link reviews into Weekly Underwrites only when the review is explicitly attached to that same Snapshot.
