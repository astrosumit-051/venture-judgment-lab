# Research the Summer 2027 early-stage investing internship market

Type: research
Status: resolved
Parent: ../map.md

## Question

Which US early-stage technology investing internships and accelerator roles could qualify for Summer 2027, when do they actually recruit, what evidence do they require, and how do F-1 CPT, later STEM OPT, and sponsorship constraints affect the realistic target universe?

## Answer

Summer 2027 recruiting is already active, so the Internship Readiness Gate moves to August 5, 2026 and applications must run alongside development. Bessemer's paid Summer Analyst role is open and exactly matches the learner's graduation year; Pear Fellows closes August 9 and Dorm Room Fund's Kentucky-region investment-partner application closes September 11, but those are Investing Milestones rather than paid Qualifying Internships. Insight's 2027 class is already closed, proving March 2027 was unsafe.

For Summer 2027, Centre-authorized CPT is the expected work-authorization path and must be confirmed before work begins. For post-graduation STEM OPT, target firms must eventually be screened for E-Verify, Form I-983, a bona fide training relationship, and work that can be documented as related to the qualifying STEM degree. Longer-term sponsorship is a separate filter.

Full evidence, classifications, application prompts, official links, and follow-up actions are in the [research asset](../../../research/summer-2027-vc-internship-market.md).
