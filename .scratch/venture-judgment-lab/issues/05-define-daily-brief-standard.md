## Question

What source-quality, freshness, balance, copyright, time-budget, teaching-purpose, deduplication, link-preservation, and date-stamping rules should govern the four daily readings and their permanent Reading Record?

## Comments

## Answer

Use the dated [`Daily Brief Curation and Reading Record Standard`](../../../research/daily-brief-curation-and-reading-record-standard.md): four distinct lanes must fit within 55 minutes, each selection must pass explicit source-quality, teaching-purpose, access, freshness, balance, deduplication, copyright, and preservation gates, and every assignment must be written as immutable metadata before delivery. Learner responses, corrections, source failures, replacements, revisits, coach feedback, linked artifacts, and later usefulness are appended as dated events without rewriting the original record.
